QFlix is moving to new, faster hardware. Nothing about the movies and shows
changes. What does change is the server's identity inside Plex, so you will
notice a few things:

  - A NEW QFlix server appears in your Plex app.
  - The OLD QFlix server disappears after we switch over.
  - Your pinned and favorited libraries start out blank on the new one.

That is normal, and it is a two-minute fix.

RE-PIN YOUR LIBRARIES

Plex Web (computer)
  Open https://app.plex.tv/desktop/ . In the left sidebar, find the new QFlix
  server and click the pin icon next to each library you want (Movies, TV, and
  so on). Use the three-dot menu to reorder them.

iPhone, iPad and Android
  Open Plex, tap More (or your profile), then Manage Libraries (sometimes
  called Edit Sidebar). Switch on the libraries from the new QFlix server and
  drag them where you like.

TV apps (Roku, Fire TV, Apple TV, Android TV, smart TVs)
  Open Plex and scroll the left menu to the bottom. Choose Edit (or press the
  options button) and pin the libraries from the new QFlix server. If the app
  still shows only the old server, close Plex completely and reopen it.

CLEARING OUT THE OLD SERVER

Once we switch over, the old QFlix entry should vanish by itself. If it
lingers on one of your devices, sign out of Plex and back in, or remove it
from Settings > Authorized Devices on Plex Web. It is just a leftover label.

ONE HONEST HEADS-UP

Watch history on the old server does not carry over. The new server starts
with a fresh slate, so shows will not remember where you left off and
"watched" marks start over. Your requests and your account are untouched.

QFlix is moving to new, faster hardware. Nothing about the movies and shows changes. What does change is the server's identity inside Plex, so you will notice a few things:

  • A new QFlix server appears in your Plex app.
  • The old QFlix server disappears after we switch over.
  • Your pinned and favorited libraries start out blank on the new one.

That is normal, and it is a two-minute fix. Here is how.

Re-pin your libraries

Plex Web (computer)

Open app.plex.tv. In the left sidebar, find the new QFlix server and click the pin icon next to each library you want (Movies, TV, and so on). Use the three-dot menu to reorder them.

iPhone, iPad and Android

Open Plex, tap More (or your profile), then Manage Libraries (sometimes called Edit Sidebar). Switch on the libraries from the new QFlix server and drag them where you like.

TV apps (Roku, Fire TV, Apple TV, Android TV, smart TVs)

Open Plex and scroll the left menu to the bottom. Choose Edit (or press the options button) and pin the libraries from the new QFlix server. If the app still shows only the old server, close Plex completely and reopen it.

Clearing out the old server

Once we switch over, the old QFlix entry should vanish by itself. If it lingers on one of your devices, sign out of Plex and back in, or remove it from Settings, Authorized Devices on Plex Web. Do not worry about it; it is just a leftover label.

One honest heads-up

Watch history on the old server does not carry over. The new server starts with a fresh slate, so shows will not remember where you left off and "watched" marks start over. Your requests and your account are untouched.

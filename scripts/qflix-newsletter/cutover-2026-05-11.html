How to use it
Three things, one minute each
1
Stream on Plex like normal.
Nothing changed for watching. Use the Plex app you already have. If you'd lost your login, sign in with the same email you've always used.
▶ Open Plex
2
Request anything you want, in Seerr.
Search the title, tap Request. We'll find it, download it, hardlink it into Plex, and you'll see it under Recently Added usually within an hour. Anime titles route to the anime libraries automatically.
+ Open Seerr
3
Skim Monday's email for what's new.
Every Monday morning you'll get a short email: Pick of the Week, new movies, new TV, new anime, what's coming soon, plus three AI suggestions at the bottom ("if you liked X, try Y"). Unsubscribe link at the bottom of every one. No hard feelings.
No login required
Glance at the dashboard.
Public-facing landing page shows what's newly added, what's trending on the box, and quick links to everything above. Bookmark it.
★  Open the dashboard
FAQ
A few things worth knowing
Will my Plex login change?
No. Same Plex SSO you've always used.
 
I used to request through Ombi — what happened?
Ombi has been retired. Everything you need is in Seerr (button above) — your past requests carry over.
 
Monday-morning emails — will it spam me?
Once a week, max. Unsubscribe link at the bottom of every one. No marketing, ever.
 
Something's broken — wrong audio, can't play, weird subtitles?
Open an issue in Seerr right from the title's page — there's a little flag/report icon on each item. I see those automatically and they kick off a re-grab. For anything else (your account, the dashboard, the newsletter), just reply to this email.
